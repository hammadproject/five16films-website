LET LOVE TAKE FLIGHT

Description

Olivia doesn't believe in love until a chance flight places her beside André, a charming stranger who sees her sharp tongue as a strength, not a flaw.

Apples and Airplanes is a slow burn romantic comedy about love, timing, and the stories we never stop waiting for.
